# Property Registration - Delhi

## 1. What is registration?
Registration means the registration of documents. Registration is made under the provisions of the Registration Act, 1908.

## 2. Document Types

### Sale Deed
* **Stamp Duty and Transfer Duty:** 4% if the vendee is a woman and 6% if the vendee is a man.
* **Registration Fee:** 1% of the total value of Sale Deed + Rs. 100/- pasting charge.
* **Requirements:** Both vendor and vendee must be present along with identification documents (i.e., Passport, Epic Card, Driving License, Pan Card, etc.) at the time of presentation. Two witnesses along with identification documents also need to be present during the presentation of documents.

### WILL
* **Stamp Duty:** No stamp duty is required for the registration of a will.
* **Registration Fee:** Rs. 600/-
* **Requirements:** The Testator along with two witnesses need to be present during presentation.

### Conveyance Deeds
* **Details:** For conversion of leasehold property into freehold property.
* **Stamp Duty:** 6% in the case of men and 4% in the case of women has to be paid through the Collector of Stamps/SDM.
* **Registration Fee:** 1% of the total value of Conveyance Deed + Rs. 100/- pasting charge has to be deposited at the Sub-Registrar office at the time of registration.

### Gift Deeds
* **Details:** The property being gifted has to be valued by an approved valuer.
* **Stamp Duty and Transfer Duty:** 4% if the donee is a woman and 6% if the donee is a man.
* **Registration Fee:** 1% of the total value + Rs. 100/- pasting charges.

### Share Certificates
* **Stamp Duty:** Re. 1/- for every one thousand rupees or part thereof of the value of shares has to be paid at the office of Collector of Stamps/SDM.

*Note: Registration fee of more than Rs. 500/- will be accepted by demand draft/pay order.*

## 3. Where to apply?
You have to apply to the Sub-Registrar (SR) Office of the concerned district.

## 4. What is the response time?
Normally within 15 days.
