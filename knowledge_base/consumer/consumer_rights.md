# Consumer Rights

## Q1. When was the Act enacted?
- Notified: 9 August 2019
- Effective: 20 July 2020

## Q2. Main features
- E-commerce & Direct Selling
- CCPA
- Misleading advertisement provisions
- Product liability
- Revised pecuniary jurisdiction
- Easier dispute resolution
- Unfair contract provisions
- Mediation

## Q5. Six Rights
- Right to Safety
- Right to be Informed
- Right to Choose
- Right to be Heard
- Right to Seek Redressal
- Right to Consumer Awareness
