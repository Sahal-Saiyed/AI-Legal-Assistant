# Consumer Definition

## Q3. Who is a consumer?
A consumer is a person who buys goods or services for consideration (paid, promised, partly paid or under deferred payment). The definition also includes the approved user/beneficiary.

Purchases covered:
- Offline
- Online
- Electronic transactions
- Teleshopping
- Direct selling
- Multi-level marketing

## Q4. Who is NOT a consumer?
- Person obtaining goods free of charge
- Person availing services free of charge
- Person buying goods for resale
- Person buying goods/services for commercial purposes
- Person availing services under a contract of service

Exception:
Commercial purpose does not include goods used exclusively for earning livelihood through self-employment.

## Q6. What are goods?
Goods mean every kind of movable property and include food under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.

## Q7. Who can make a complaint?
- Consumer
- Registered consumer association
- Central/State Government
- Central Consumer Protection Authority
- One or more consumers with common interest
- Legal heir/legal representative
- Parent/guardian of a minor consumer

## Q8. Can business purchasers file?
Generally No, except the self-employment livelihood exception above.
