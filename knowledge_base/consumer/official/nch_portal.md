# National Consumer Helpline (NCH)

## Purpose
NCH is an integrated pre-litigation grievance redress mechanism (INGRAM) for consumer grievances.

## What grievances?
Consumer-related grievances.

## Registration
- Call 1800-11-4000 or 1915
- Register on consumerhelpline.gov.in
- SMS 8800001915
- NCH App
- UMANG App

## Tracking
Track using the docket number on the portal.

## Processing
Complaints are forwarded to the concerned company/authority. Typical target is up to 30 days.

## If unresolved
Consumer may approach the appropriate Consumer Commission.
