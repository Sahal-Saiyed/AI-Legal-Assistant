# Complaint Procedure

## Q35. Filing procedure
- Complaint should be in writing.
- Offline or online (e-Daakhil).
- Can be filed personally, through agent or by registered post.
- Normally three copies plus copies for each opposite party.

## Q40. Required particulars
- Complainant details
- Opposite party details
- Facts of complaint
- Supporting documents

## Q41. Limitation
Normally within two years from cause of action.

## Q42 & Q45. Fees
Court fee payable as prescribed. Amount depends on value of goods/services.

## Q44. Appeals
District → State (30 days)
State → National (30 days)
National → Supreme Court (45 days)
